# Apexiar Website Instructions

## Restructure control document

- Before restructure work, read [WEBSITE-RESTRUCTURE-PLAN.md](WEBSITE-RESTRUCTURE-PLAN.md) for the current phase, migration map, protected content and review gates.
- Update its phase tracker, affected migration rows and progress log after every restructure task; record checks, deviations and the next action.
- Preserve the homepage's rising glowing pixels, glowing horizon/connected-node design, complete video section, Certified & Compliant strip, customer testimonial, and Built for Regulated Industries section (including its mission and statistics), as specified in the control document.
- Work only within the requested phase or page and stop at its review gate.

## Working approach

- Inspect the existing implementation before changing files.
- Preserve existing content, imagery, product cards and functionality unless explicitly authorised to remove them.
- Where content is removed from one page, identify and implement its new destination first.
- Work in small, reviewable phases.
- Do not undertake a full-site rewrite without explicit approval.
- Do not introduce a new framework or production dependency without approval.
- Preserve existing product URLs wherever possible.
- Do not rename or relocate assets without checking every reference.
- Maintain the existing black, blue and gold Apexiar visual identity.
- Maintain the current premium, enterprise and technology-led design language.
- Avoid generic AI imagery and generic software-agency language.

## Quality requirements

- Test desktop, tablet and mobile layouts.
- Run the existing build, lint and test commands after each phase.
- Check internal links, product links and asset paths.
- Preserve accessibility, semantic headings and metadata.
- Report the files changed and tests completed after every task.
- Do not commit changes unless requested.
