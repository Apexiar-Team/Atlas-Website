Engineering beyond the original design

Recover capability. Extend asset life.

Apexiar combines rail engineering, digital assurance and deployed condition-monitoring technology to extend asset life, recover obsolete systems and unlock new operational intelligence.

01 / RECONSTRUCT

Precision re-engineering

Using 3D scanning, dimensional capture and modern CAD, Apexiar can reconstruct components where drawings, tooling or original suppliers are no longer available.

  • Technical investigation and reverse engineering
  • Rapid prototyping and design for manufacture
  • Physical testing and controlled engineering change
02 / RESTORE

Overhaul & lifecycle extension

Assessment, overhaul and verification of selected auxiliary and electromechanical equipment, including HVAC systems, alternators and commutators.

  • Failure investigation and component recovery
  • Specialist tooling and test-rig development
  • Modification packages and technical evidence to support return to service
03 / LEARN

Close the engineering loop

Extend condition monitoring to equipment that was never designed to generate data. Use asset telemetry to inform maintenance, overhaul and the next engineering decision.

Collaborative development

Conceive. Prototype. Prove. Deploy.

Work alongside our research and development team to define the outcome, test ideas early and generate evidence for the route to approvals, integration and implementation.

  1. 01

    Conceive

    Define the operational challenge, engineering requirements and constraints with operators, asset owners and delivery partners.

  2. 02

    Prototype

    Combine software, applied AI, edge sensing and engineering methods to test the idea quickly and reduce development risk.

  3. 03

    Prove

    Test and validate against the required safety, reliability and operational demands. Build usable evidence for assurance and required approvals.

  4. 04

    Deploy

    Plan integration and implementation around the operation, supported by training, handover and ongoing maintenance.
