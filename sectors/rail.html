Connected Systems / Development programmes

Intelligence at vehicle and track level.

Train Node and Track Node are developing sensing programmes. Their intended uses and engineering targets are distinct from achieved approvals or guaranteed availability.

In development & testing

Train Node

Vehicle-mounted sensing for acceleration, vibration, orientation, location and rail-condition indicators, with edge processing and journey records.

Engineering validation and required railway approvals are targeted for the end of 2026; initial market testing is planned for Q1 2027. These are programme targets.

Train Node programme & optional modules →

Subject to development & field trials

Track Node

Track-mounted sensing intended to capture movement, vibration, orientation, temperature and environmental context at the infrastructure.

Final mounting, sensing, power and communications configurations remain subject to development, field trials and railway approval.

Aerial Intelligence

Inspect the corridor.
Prioritise the work.

Use repeat aerial surveys to build vegetation workbanks and support verification of completed work.

Visual and thermal inspection can support assessment of track, structures, earthworks, drainage, vegetation and overhead line equipment, with remote reconnaissance for incident response.

The Gen 1 UAV configuration is operational; Gen 2 remains in engineering development. Aircraft, payloads and integration are defined around the inspection requirement.
